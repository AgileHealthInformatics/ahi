## About this page

The **About** page describes Agile Health Informatics Ltd, its professional focus and the experience of **Dr Tito Castillo**.

It provides background for organisations considering the practice for work involving enterprise architecture, health data, interoperability, secure environments, digital transformation and related assurance.

## What the page covers

The page presents four recurring characteristics of the practice:

- strategy combined with technical depth;
- clinical and research context;
- data treated as infrastructure; and
- assurance that extends through delivery.

It also describes professional experience across national programmes, provider transformation, research infrastructure, shared records, regulated software, predictive analytics and health data architecture.
