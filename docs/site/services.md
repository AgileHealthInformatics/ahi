# Agile Health Informatics Services

[Open the Services page](../../services.html)

## About this page

The **Services** page describes the six complementary areas of practice offered by Agile Health Informatics Ltd.

The services are aimed particularly at healthcare problems that cross organisational or disciplinary boundaries and require both strategic judgement and technical depth.

## Service areas

### Enterprise architecture

Supports complex transformation decisions through capability models, target operating models, architecture assurance, repositories and roadmaps.

### Health data architecture

Treats data as infrastructure, covering metadata, standards, semantic interoperability, information models, governance and reuse.

### Interoperability

Addresses clinical information flows, integration, FHIR, HL7, IHE, terminology and cross-organisational exchange.

### Secure data environments

Combines architecture, information governance and security for controlled use of sensitive health and research data.

### AI-enabled architecture

Uses AI to accelerate analysis, modelling and publication while retaining professional judgement and assurance.

### Transformation readiness

Examines the organisational, workflow, data and architecture conditions required for successful implementation, including EPR readiness and clinical pathway dependencies.

## Delivery approach

The page describes a four-stage working method:

1. **Understand the problem**
2. **Make the system visible**
3. **Test coherence and risk**
4. **Leave usable assets**

This reflects an approach in which architecture artefacts are used to clarify decisions and support delivery rather than being produced as documentation for its own sake.

## How to use the page

Use the page to:

- identify which service area best fits the problem;
- understand the relationship between the service areas;
- compare the work with examples of prior healthcare experience; and
- start a discussion about a specific architecture, data or transformation problem.

## Scope

The service descriptions are intentionally overlapping because many healthcare transformation problems span architecture, data, interoperability, governance, security and delivery at the same time.
