UK Secondary Care Integration Reference Atlas

A capability-led comparison of the integration mechanisms and data flows that connect secondary care with national, primary-care and shared-record services in England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland. It also compares the four nations with the emerging EHDS architecture without treating architectural alignment as legal compliance.
