Theatres pathway navigator
Reference catalogue, not clinical guidance. Every definition is draft and unapproved, for analysis and service design only. Conformance to HPDS does not imply clinical correctness or approval.

Show links: Select a pathway to highlight its links. Select it again to open it.

Evidence and impact

Select a source to see which pathway definitions depend on it, directly or through a shared sub-pathway. This is the question a guidance producer asks before an update. Links are to guidance as a whole unless a recommendation reference is recorded on the pathway.

Pathway variants

Each variant specialises a reference definition and records its deviations. The reference definitions state the legal framework and care setting as parameters, so they stay valid across jurisdictions and settings. Values are as at the date shown.
