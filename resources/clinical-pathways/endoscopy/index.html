Reference catalogue, not clinical guidance. Every definition is draft and unapproved, for analysis and service design only. Conformance to HPDS does not imply clinical correctness or approval.

Evidence and impact

Select a source to see which pathway definitions depend on it, directly or through a shared sub-pathway. This is the question a guidance producer asks before an update. Links are to guidance as a whole unless a recommendation reference is recorded on the pathway.

Jurisdictional variants

The reference definition leaves the threshold and age range as parameters set by each national programme. Each variant specialises it and records the deviation, so the reference stays valid in every jurisdiction. Values are as at the date shown and are changing during the England rollout.

Choose a role, then a pathway in which that role is involved. The view brings the catalogue’s entry, activities, decisions, information and exits together around that stakeholder’s work, making hand-offs easier to examine.
