What follows in an operational EHDS service?

1

Discover metadata in a national or European catalogue.

2

Apply through the appropriate access-application process.

3

Assess purpose, legal grounds, safeguards and feasibility.

4

Provide controlled access only where authorised, commonly through an approved secure processing environment.

Important boundary: An enquiry in this demonstration never becomes a real data-access application or permit.

06 / Standards and implementation boundaries

Which standards belong where?

The demonstration separates catalogue discovery, the metadata exchange model, publication governance and the authorisation of data use.

Foundation

DCAT and DCAT-AP

RDF modelling of catalogues, datasets, records, distributions and agents. Interoperable descriptions support discovery and metadata exchange.

Read DCAT-AP ↗
Health-specific profile

HealthDCAT-AP Release 8

Health-specific metadata and access categories for secondary use. A full implementation requires cardinalities, required vocabularies and applicable validation constraints.

Read HealthDCAT-AP ↗
EU legal framework

European Health Data Space

National dataset catalogue obligations and the arrangements for secondary-use discovery, access applications and secure processing remain distinct capabilities.

Read EHDS Regulation ↗
