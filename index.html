How we work

Structured enough to assure. Agile enough to be useful.

The practice is built around evidence, traceability and pragmatic delivery. Architecture methods are used to clarify decisions and reduce risk, not to impose process for its own sake.

1

Understand the problem

Establish the decision context, stakeholders, constraints, existing evidence and the operational consequences of getting the answer wrong.

2

Make the system visible

Use capability, process, information and dependency models to expose relationships that are often hidden inside programmes and supplier artefacts.

3

Test coherence and risk

Examine whether strategy, data, technology, governance, clinical workflow and delivery plans actually align, and make uncertainty explicit.

4

Leave usable assets

Produce traceable models, catalogues, specifications, decision records and implementation guidance that can continue to support delivery after the engagement.
